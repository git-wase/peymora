# Website Development Prompt — AI-Powered Smart Dog Collar

## Project Overview

Build a premium, minimalist product landing page for a new AI-powered smart dog collar designed to detect and discourage unwanted digging behavior.

Use https://teelicht.pro/ as the primary design inspiration, particularly its minimalist aesthetic, premium product presentation, typography, product imagery, and elegant technical storytelling.

The website's primary purpose is **market validation and email lead generation**, not immediate product sales.

The product is currently in development. The website should generate excitement, communicate the product's value proposition, and encourage prospective customers to join an early-access interest list.

Do not copy the reference website's branding, images, or proprietary content.

## 1. Product Concept

The product is a smart dog collar equipped with motion sensors and machine-learning technology capable of identifying digging behavior.

Unlike conventional training collars that require an owner to observe their dog and manually initiate a correction, this collar is being developed to detect digging behavior automatically.

The collar's intended functionality includes:

- **AI-Powered Digging Detection:** Motion sensors monitor a dog's movements, and an onboard machine-learning model identifies movement patterns associated with digging.
- **Automatic Behavior Response:** When digging is detected, the collar can initiate an owner-configured response without requiring immediate manual intervention.
- **Audible Correction:** An audible cue can be triggered to interrupt digging behavior.
- **Vibration Correction:** A vibration-based cue offers another non-electrical correction option.
- **Optional Electrical Stimulation:** For owners who are comfortable with this approach, the proposed product may support adjustable electrical stimulation. This feature must be explicitly enabled by the owner and should never be presented as required for effective use.
- **Customizable Training Preferences:** Owners can choose their preferred response method, with an emphasis on control, responsible training, and animal welfare.

Position the collar as an intelligent training assistant that helps dog owners address unwanted digging behavior while maintaining control over how their dog receives feedback.

The technology is under development. Do not imply that detection accuracy, correction effectiveness, safety certifications, or commercial availability have already been established.

**Subheadline:**
"Meet the smart collar being developed to recognize digging behavior and respond automatically, with training cues you control."

**Primary CTA:**
"Join the Early Access List"

### Section 2 — The Problem

**Headline:**
"Your Dog Doesn't Wait Until You're Watching."

Explain that unwanted digging can damage lawns, gardens, and landscaping, and that owners cannot always be present to interrupt the behavior.

Use concise copy and compelling imagery to establish the problem.

### Section 3 — The Solution

**Headline:**
"A Smarter Way to Address Digging."

Explain the proposed system in three simple steps:

1. **Detect:** Onboard motion sensors observe movement patterns associated with digging.
2. **Recognize:** Machine-learning algorithms analyze those patterns to identify potential digging behavior.
3. **Respond:** The collar delivers the owner's selected training cue when appropriate.

Create an elegant visual flow showing detection, recognition, and response.

Make clear that detection is being developed and validated, rather than presenting the system as a proven finished product.

### Section 4 — Training Options

**Headline:**
"Your Dog. Your Training Preferences."

Display three premium feature cards:

**Audible Cue**
A simple sound designed to interrupt unwanted behavior.

**Vibration Cue**
A configurable vibration provides another training option.

**Optional Electrical Stimulation**
An additional owner-controlled option for those comfortable with it, subject to appropriate safety controls and product validation.

Emphasize that owners select the response methods they are comfortable using.

Do not depict stimulation as painless, harmless, or universally recommended. Do not imply any correction method is guaranteed to work.

### Section 5 — Technology

**Headline:**
"Intelligence Built Into Every Movement."

Showcase the underlying engineering concepts:

- Motion sensing
- Machine-learning-based behavior recognition
- On-device processing
- Configurable behavior responses
- Compact wearable electronics

Use visually engaging diagrams, sensor illustrations, or animated motion-data graphics.

Keep explanations accessible to nontechnical dog owners while communicating genuine engineering sophistication.

Avoid inventing technical specifications such as battery life, waterproof ratings, wireless connectivity, or detection accuracy.

### Section 6 — Designed for Everyday Life

**Headline:**
"Made for Dogs. Designed for Their Humans."

Feature lifestyle imagery of dogs in backyards and outdoor environments.

Focus on the intended benefits of convenient, customizable training assistance.

Use realistic product render placeholders rather than pretending that concept imagery represents finished hardware.

### Section 7 — Early Access Email Signup

**Headline:**
"Be First to Know."

**Subheadline:**
"We're developing a smarter approach to digging prevention. Join our early-access list for product updates, development milestones, and launch information."

Create a prominent, beautifully designed email signup form.

Required field:
- Email address

Optional fields:
- Dog size: Small / Medium / Large
- How often does your dog dig? Occasionally / Frequently / Daily
- Which correction methods would you consider? Audible / Vibration / Electrical stimulation / Unsure

The form should prioritize email conversion. Keep optional research questions secondary and do not require them to subscribe.

**Functional requirements:**

- Store email signups in a persistent database or connected email-marketing platform.
- Use a real backend integration, not a mock form.
- Validate email addresses.
- Prevent duplicate subscriptions.
- Include spam and bot protection.
- Provide a clear success confirmation.
- Include a privacy policy and consent language.
- Do not automatically enroll subscribers in unrelated marketing.
- Make subscriber data exportable to CSV.
- Support double opt-in where appropriate.
- Allow subscribers to unsubscribe from marketing emails.

If the database or email provider is not yet configured, create the integration structure and clearly identify which credentials and setup steps are required. Never claim submissions are saved unless the backend is actually working.

## 5. Market Validation and Analytics

This website is intended to measure real customer demand.

Implement privacy-conscious analytics capable of tracking:

- Unique visitors
- Email signup conversions
- Conversion rate
- Primary CTA clicks
- Engagement with the training-options section
- Responses to optional interest questions
- Traffic acquisition sources

Use a lightweight analytics platform such as Plausible or Vercel Web Analytics, with appropriate privacy and consent handling.

Make it possible to evaluate which marketing messages generate the greatest interest.

Do not display fabricated testimonials, customer counts, preorder numbers, or product reviews.

## 7. Important Product and Safety Considerations

The website must clearly distinguish proposed capabilities from validated capabilities.

Avoid unsupported claims about:

- Detection accuracy
- Training effectiveness
- Animal safety
- Battery performance
- Waterproofing
- Regulatory approval
- Availability dates

Emphasize owner control, humane training considerations, and responsible product development.

Do not suggest the collar is intended to replace supervision or professional veterinary or behavioral advice.
